# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro. The site combines marketing pages that must rank on Google with a blog of ongoing content. Astro ships static HTML by default (fast, crawlable, strong Core Web Vitals), handles Markdown/MDX content collections natively for the blog, and allows interactive islands only where needed. Deploy target not yet decided.

## Users

Primary: owners of aesthetics, health and wellness businesses (micropigmentation clinics, trichology/hair clinics, dermatologists, general practitioners, wellness brands, creators selling products), mostly led by women, in Colombia, the United States and Canada. They need a website, Google visibility and/or AI automation, and are evaluating who to trust with it. The job: decide whether Jhon is the right person and book a diagnosis.

Secondary: a community audience that reads the blog and content of value (web, Google positioning, AI automation for these businesses). Content builds trust and should lead readers toward the same single action.

## Product Purpose

jhondev is a hybrid of freelance portfolio, personal brand and small studio. It sells websites, Google positioning (SEO) and AI automation (e.g. WhatsApp + CRM) for aesthetics, health and wellness businesses. Success means qualified prospects book a diagnosis.

Single call to action across the site: **"Agenda tu diagnóstico"**.

## Positioning

- Phased process: diagnosis, written proposal, three deliveries the client approves before each payment, then launch ("encendido").
- The client talks directly to the person who builds, never a junior assigned to the account.
- Sites are born ready to rank on Google, not fixed afterward.
- Delivery in weeks using AI, not months.
- Domain and access stay in the client's name.
- Niche focus: aesthetics, health and wellness, mostly women-led businesses.

## Operating Context

Based in Bogotá, Colombia; clients in Colombia, the US and Canada. Work spans websites, SEO, and AI automation integrated with WhatsApp and CRM. Engagement starts with a diagnosis, followed by a written proposal and staged approvals tied to payments.

## Product Principles

1. One path: every page leads to "Agenda tu diagnóstico".
2. Trust through transparency: show the process, approvals and ownership terms plainly.
3. Proof over promise: use only real cases and real reviews; leave gaps visible rather than invent numbers.
4. Practise what is sold: the site itself must be fast and built to rank on Google.
5. Speak to the niche: aesthetics, health and wellness owners, not generic businesses.
