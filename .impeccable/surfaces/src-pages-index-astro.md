## Scope
Home (src/pages/index.astro). Mode: Persuade. Spanish launch; ES|EN structure ready, EN in phase 2. Single action: "Agenda tu diagnóstico" (Cal.com link, placeholder until the user supplies the event URL).

## Audience and proof
Owners of aesthetics/health/wellness clinics, mobile first. Proof: 7 real cases (Doctora Capilar, 10 months in Google, leads), 36 five-star Google reviews (only real texts from women clients, provisional selection until the user's list), phased process with approval before each payment, domain in the client's name. No invented figures, photos or testimonials.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is a dermocosmetic product label: the information system the client reads daily on bottles and boxes. Refuses the dark-neon dev-agency hero and the beige-serif spa page.
OWN-WORLD: White page everywhere; the label as the one object on a white stage, ruled in #222 with label field names; red #FF002B only for the power icon and the action, always on white; logo kept as-is (white wordmark, red power O) on a #222 strip in header and footer. Hanken Grotesk uppercase for titles, Montserrat for body and composition lines; body ≥16px, captions 13px #555555.
STORY: She reads the H1 and knows it is for her clinic, sees the warnings she lives with, finds her clinic type, reads what the formula contains, sees real cases and reviews, understands she approves before paying, and books a diagnosis.
FIRST VIEWPORT: H1 "Sitios web y posicionamiento en Google para clínicas de estética y salud" across the top on white; below it the white label object with the product name and the red "Agenda tu diagnóstico" button plus "36 reseñas en Google" stars in its top band, then Indicado para / Composición / Modo de uso rows; leader-line callouts outside the label with "36 reseñas · 5,0" and "10 meses en Google · Doctora Capilar". On mobile the H1 and button with stars sit inside the first viewport.
